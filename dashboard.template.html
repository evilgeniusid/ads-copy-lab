Ads copy tag lab

Every live headline, long headline and description is tagged by attribute, then joined to Google Ads asset metrics. Read non-branded for decisions. Branded CTR is inflated by brand queries.

* under 1,000 impressions, directional only. Asset metrics are "served with" metrics, so lift is correlation. Confirm a winner with a same-ad-group A/B before changing standing rules. Tags on legacy assets come from a regex tagger. New copy is tagged by the writer at generation time.
